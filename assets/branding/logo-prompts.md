# Topova AI logo concept v1

Exploration: built-in ImageGen. Final clean masters: native SVG geometry with outlined type, reconstructed from the concept. Transparent PNG previews are rendered from the SVG masters. The approved SVG identity is integrated through frontend/src/components/brand/topova-logo.tsx. Runtime assets are published from frontend/public/brand, with matching favicon and Apple touch icon.

## Primary exploration prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original, professional primary logo for the existing brand "Topova AI", a network topology design and AI engineering platform. This is a new brand identity proposal, not a variation of a stock technology icon.
Asset: a clean horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, centered with generous but not excessive clear space. Large high-resolution, crisp edges, vector-friendly flat graphic.
Symbol: a confident, distinctive compact geometric monogram suggesting a capital T and network routing/topology through clever negative space. Integrate at most two small endpoint nodes into the structure, with a single purposeful path. Strong recognizable silhouette that remains legible as a 24px favicon. Design it like a considered enterprise software identity by a top brand studio. Balanced optical proportions, disciplined geometry, no fine intricate wiring.
Wordmark exactly: "Topova AI" (T-o-p-o-v-a, space A-I). Refined custom-looking contemporary sans-serif typography, semibold, excellent kerning, friendly precise shapes. Topova is the main name; AI slightly lighter in weight, on the same baseline. Icon left, wordmark right.
Palette: symbol uses a restrained electric cobalt blue and teal accent, solid flat fills; wordmark deep ink navy. Legible and polished on a white or light background. No background rectangle.
Constraints: one finished logo only. No presentation board, no extra variants, no explanatory labels, no tagline, no mockup, no texture, no lighting, no 3D, no gradients, no shadows, no glow. Avoid hexagonal shield outlines, AI sparkles, brain symbols, generic globes, clipart network diagrams, and overly complex circuit boards. Preserve actual alpha transparency.
